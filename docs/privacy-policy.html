Privacy Policy

Notification Spy — Last updated: November 23, 2026

Overview

Notification Spy (package com.darkapps.notificationspy) is a utility app developed by Anton Priadko (“the developer”) that forwards Android notifications to a webhook URL you configure. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data the app accesses

The app uses Android's Notification Listener permission to read notifications from apps you explicitly select. This includes the notification title, body text, and source app name.

Where your data goes

Notification content is sent exclusively to the webhook URL you provide in the app settings. The app has no backend server of its own — your data never passes through any infrastructure controlled by the developer. You choose this destination; the developer has no access to it and no knowledge of what you configure.

Local storage

The app stores a local history of forwarded notifications on your device (title, body, delivery status, timestamp). This data does not leave your device except via the webhook you configured.

Camera

The app requests camera access only so you can scan a QR code containing your webhook configuration. Camera images are processed on the device in real time and are never stored or transmitted.

Third parties and sharing

The webhook endpoint you configure is operated by you or by a third party you choose. Once notification content is delivered to that endpoint, its handling is governed by that operator's policies, not this one. The developer does not receive, collect or share your data.

Security

Notification content is sent over the network to your webhook URL. The app only accepts HTTPS webhook URLs, so content is encrypted in transit. Local history is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access on a non-rooted device.

Data retention and deletion

Local history is automatically deleted from your device after 30 days. You can also remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. The developer holds no copy of your data and so has nothing to delete on a server.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send data to the developer or any third-party server — only to the webhook URL you configure and control
  • It does not include analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs
  • It does not access notifications from apps you have not explicitly enabled
  • It does not store notification content in any cloud service

Your control

You can revoke notification access at any time in Android system settings (Settings → Notifications → Notification access). Removing the app deletes all locally stored history.
